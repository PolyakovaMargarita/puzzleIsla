Common questions

How do I continue after failing a level?
You can watch a short rewarded ad for extra moves, or continue free if you purchased Remove Ads.

How do I remove ads?
Open Settings and choose Remove Ads. You can Restore Purchases on a new device from the same Apple ID.

I lost my progress.
Progress is stored locally on the device. Reinstalling or switching devices does not sync progress. If something unexpected happened, email us with details.
