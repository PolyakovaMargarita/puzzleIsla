Puzzle Isle

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

Overview

Puzzle Isle (“the App”) is developed by Margarita Polyakova. This policy explains what information the App uses and how.

You do not need an account to play. Core game progress is stored on your device.

Data stored on your device

  • Game progress (levels completed, restoration stars, island décor placement)
  • Settings (language, music, sound, vibration, purchase unlock status)

This information stays on your device and is not uploaded to our own servers. We do not operate a Puzzle Isle account backend.

Advertising

The App may show rewarded and interstitial ads through Google AdMob. Ad partners may collect device identifiers and usage data to deliver and measure ads, according to their own policies.

You can purchase Remove Ads to skip full-screen ads. Rewarded continues may remain available without watching an ad after that purchase.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store). We do not receive your full payment card details. Purchase status may be stored on the device so Remove Ads stays unlocked, and can be restored with Restore Purchases.

Children

Puzzle Isle is designed as a casual family-friendly game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for our own use. Advertising partners may process limited device data as described above. Parents can limit tracking in device settings and may purchase Remove Ads.

Third parties

  • Google AdMob — advertising
  • Apple / Google — app distribution and in-app purchases

We do not sell your personal information.
